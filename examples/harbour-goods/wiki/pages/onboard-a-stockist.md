# Onboard a stockist

How a new shop starts stocking [[harbour-goods]].

1. Shop enquires. Send the wholesale price list and the [[minimum-order-150]] rule the same day.
2. First order: 150 units minimum, paid up front.
3. After the first order: 30-day terms, no minimum on reorders.
4. Create an entity page for the shop in the wiki with buyer name and first order.

Open question: whether to offer a 100-unit trial for very small shops (see [[minimum-order-150]]).
